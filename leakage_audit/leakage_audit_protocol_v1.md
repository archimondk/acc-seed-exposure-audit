# Pre-registered protocol: seed–target leakage audit for ACC-PHARMA-NET

**Protocol ID:** `leakage_audit_v1`
**Status:** DRAFT — freeze before executing any interventional arm
**Applies to:** ACC-PHARMA-NET (manuscript rev6 and successors)

---

## 1. Purpose

To determine whether the drug ordering produced by disease-seeded random-walk-with-restart
(RWR) propagation reflects disease-specific network context, or is instead determined by
whether a drug is **directly associated with a gene that was placed in the restart vector**.

We refer to the latter mechanism as **seed–target leakage**: the disease module is curated
from literature that already encodes knowledge of candidate drug targets, so a drug can
score highly for the circular reason that its associated genes were selected as seeds.

This protocol specifies (a) observational analyses that require no new computation, and
(b) two interventional arms that manipulate seed membership directly.

---

## 2. Provenance and honesty declaration

This protocol is **not** blind to prior results. It was written after observing:

- Arm A1 (ACC, existing 45 disease-only seeds) — complete
- Arm B1 (ER+/HER2− breast control, existing 24 seeds) — complete
- Observational analyses O1–O4, including the relationship between seed-overlap
  share and C_ACC percentile — complete

The leakage hypothesis was **generated by those observations**. What is pre-registered here
is the *test* of that hypothesis: the interventional arms (Section 6), the endpoints
(Section 7), the decision thresholds (Section 8), and the falsification conditions
(Section 9). No A2, B2 or sensitivity-arm result has been evaluated at the time
of freezing. O1–O4 are explicitly exploratory and cannot be used to modify the
interventional decision thresholds after freezing.

This declaration must be reproduced verbatim in the manuscript's Methods section. Presenting
the leakage finding as a pre-specified hypothesis would be inaccurate.

## 4. Definitions

**Seed set S.** The 45 disease-only ACC seeds: all 50 rows of
`data/ACC_P0.5C_gene_weights_v1.csv` minus the five therapy-response/vulnerability genes
(MGMT, SLFN11, ABCB1, SOAT1, UBA1).

**Disease-only seed weight.** `w_raw(g) = ACC_weight(g) − 0.15 × Therapeutic_relevance(g)`,
equivalently `0.30·G + 0.20·R + 0.20·P + 0.10·L + 0.05·S`. Normalized over the retained
seeds to sum to one before forming the restart vector.

**Seed-overlap share.** For drug *d* with associated gene set `assoc(d)`, `n_d = |assoc(d)|`:

```
L_d(weighted) = ( Σ_{g ∈ assoc(d) ∩ S} w(g) ) / (n_d + k)
L_d(count)    = ( |assoc(d) ∩ S| )         / (n_d + k)
```

`L_d(count)` is the pre-specified primary form: it uses no weights and is therefore the more
conservative and more easily reproduced baseline.

**Leakage exposure of an arm.** The set of seeds added or removed relative to the reference
arm; a drug is *exposed* if any of its associated genes is in that set.

## 5. Observational analyses (no new computation; specified here to fix the reporting)

**O1 — Replacement baseline.** Spearman correlation and Top-20 Jaccard overlap between the
`L_d(count)` ordering and the observed C_ACC ordering, and separately against ADRS_comp.
Reported descriptively. This re-purposes the "direct ACC-seed overlap fraction" baseline
already present in Methods §2.11, which was previously assessed only against the now-retired
substitution threshold.

**O2 — Seed-adjacency ceiling.** Among drugs with `|assoc(d) ∩ S| = 0`, report the maximum
observed C_ACC percentile and the number of Top-25% positions occupied by such drugs.
Pre-specified claim to be tested: **no drug lacking a direct seed association reaches the
Top 25%.** If true, prespecified positive-control recovery criterion 3 was structurally
unattainable for any drug without a seed association, independent of pharmacology.

**O3 — Seed / non-seed decomposition.** Split each drug's associated genes into the seed and
non-seed parts and recompute two shrinkage scores:

```
C_seed(d)    = (n_seed(d)·mean r_ACC over seed genes    + k·μ₀) / (n_seed(d) + k)
C_nonseed(d) = (n_non(d) ·mean r_ACC over non-seed genes + k·μ₀) / (n_non(d)  + k)
```

Report Spearman of each against (i) observed C_ACC percentile, (ii) the degree-matched
z score, (iii) MIPE potency percentile. Also report the partial Spearman between C_ACC and
the degree-matched z after residualizing both on ranked `L_d(count)`.

Interpretation fixed in advance: if `C_nonseed` shows no material ordering relationship and
the partial correlation is near zero, the propagation layer contributes little independent
disease-specific ordering beyond restart-vector membership. No significance threshold is
attached to O3; it is reported descriptively.

**O4 — Control-disease consistency.** Recompute `L_d` for the 108 drugs against the 24 frozen
breast-control seeds and report the `L_d`–ranking relationship, seed coverage and
Top-25% occupancy for arm B1 alongside the corresponding ACC quantities. O4 is
descriptive: no threshold or requirement that B1 reproduce the ACC ceiling is
attached to this analysis.

## 6. Interventional arms

The manipulated factor is membership of **RB1** in the restart vector. RB1 is the only G1/S
gene in the 399-gene universe that is associated with any CDK4/6 inhibitor (abemaciclib,
palbociclib); CDK4 and CDK6 are in the universe but are associated with neither.

| Arm | Disease | Seed set | n seeds | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | ACC | existing disease-only set | 45 | complete |
| **A2** | ACC | A1 minus **RB1** | 44 | to run |
| **B1** | breast control | existing frozen set | 24 | complete |
| **B2** | breast control | B1 plus **RB1** | 25 | to run |
| A3 *(optional)* | ACC | A1 minus RB1, TERT, MEN1 | 42 | optional |
| B3 *(optional)* | breast control | B1 plus RB1, CCND1 | 26 | optional |

**Weight assignment for RB1 in B2.** To avoid subjective scoring of a newly added seed, RB1
receives the **median raw weight of the 24 retained breast-control seeds**, stated as an
uninformative anchor. Sensitivity at ±50% of that value is reported. The same rule applies
to CCND1 in optional arm B3.

Arms A3/B3 are exploratory and, if run, are reported separately from the primary A2/B2
contrast. TERT and MEN1 are included in A3 because they are the highest-`L_d` seeds in the
ACC arm (pralatrexate is associated with both).

## 7. Technical requirements (mandatory; deviations invalidate the arm)

**R1 — Regenerate the null for every arm.** The degree-matched null replaces each seed with
a non-seed gene drawn from the same log₂(degree+1) bin. Changing the seed set changes the
seed count, the sampling pool (a removed seed becomes eligible as a replacement), and the
per-bin composition. Each arm therefore requires a **freshly generated set of 10,000 matched
seed sets using RNG seed 20260727**. Reusing the existing draws is prohibited.

**R2 — Renormalize and report the perturbation.** After removal or addition, the retained
seed weights are renormalized to sum to one. Report the normalized restart weight of the
manipulated gene in each arm, so the residual perturbation to the other seeds is quantified.

**R3 — All four normalization variants.** Every arm is run under all four variants using the
same 10,000 matched seed sets within that arm.

**R4 — Unchanged elsewhere.** Graph, 399-gene projection, 108-drug universe, α, k, μ₀, bin
definitions, and RNG seed are identical across arms. No other component may be modified.

**R5 — Manifests.** Each arm records input SHA-256 hashes, output SHA-256 hashes, OS,
machine and processor identifiers, logical CPU count, and end-to-end wall-clock time.

## 8. Endpoints and decision rules

Comparison uses the degree-matched **z score**, which is standardized within each arm's own
null and is therefore comparable across arms. Empirical P and BH q are reported alongside but
are not used for the primary contrast.

**Primary endpoints** (abemaciclib, primary `column_minmax` normalization):

```
Δz_ACC    = z_A1(abemaciclib) − z_A2(abemaciclib)      [removal effect]
Δz_Breast = z_B2(abemaciclib) − z_B1(abemaciclib)      [addition effect]
```

**Decision rules, all four required for a leakage verdict:**

| ID | Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | Δz_ACC | ≥ 2.0 |
| **L2** | Δz_Breast | ≥ 1.5 |
| **L3** | Effect symmetry: \|Δz_ACC − Δz_Breast\| | ≤ 2.0 |
| **L4** | L1–L3 hold in the same direction | ≥ 3 of 4 normalizations |

If L1–L4 all hold, the CDK4/6 signal in the ACC arm is attributed to seed–target leakage.
The lower B-arm threshold reflects its substantially lower seed coverage
(14/108 drugs with at least one B1 seed association versus 46/108 in A1);
the wider symmetry tolerance is fixed before A2/B2 execution for the same
design reason.

**Secondary endpoints, reported for all arms × all four normalizations:**

- CDK4/6 group mean C_ACC percentile and upper-tail empirical P
- palbociclib z (expected smaller shift than abemaciclib: RB1 is 1 of 26 associated genes
  versus 1 of 5)
- number of drugs passing BH q < 0.05
- rank of pralatrexate and pemetrexed

## 9. Internal negative control and falsification

**NC1 — Ribociclib.** Ribociclib has no association with RB1 or with any other manipulated
seed. Its z must not change materially:

```
|z_A1(ribociclib) − z_A2(ribociclib)| ≤ 0.5
|z_B2(ribociclib) − z_B1(ribociclib)| ≤ 0.5
```

Failure of NC1 indicates that renormalization alone is shifting scores. In that case an
**equal-mass control arm** is added: RB1 is replaced by a degree-matched non-seed,
non-cell-cycle gene carrying the same weight, holding seed count and total restart mass
constant, and the primary contrast is re-derived against that arm.

**NC2 — Unexposed drugs.** Among the 108 drugs, those with no association to any manipulated
seed should show |Δz| ≤ 0.5. Report the distribution.

**F1 — Falsification.** If `z_A2(abemaciclib) ≥ 2.0`, the leakage hypothesis is rejected for
the ACC arm: the signal survives removal of the shared seed and has another source, which
must then be traced. This outcome is to be reported with the same prominence as a positive
result.

**F2 — Falsification.** If O2 fails (i.e. drugs with no seed association do reach the
Top 25%), the seed-adjacency ceiling claim is withdrawn.

## 11. Reporting

Regardless of outcome, the following are reported:

1. All arms and all four normalizations; no arm may be omitted.
2. The provenance declaration in Section 2.
3. The asymmetry between the ACC seed set (contains RB1, CDKN2A, CCNE1, CDK4) and the
   breast-control seed rule (force-excludes CDK4/CDK6; RB1 excluded by the ≥5% prevalence
   threshold). This asymmetry must be disclosed in Methods §2.3 and §2.12, not only in the
   Discussion.
4. The correction that the manuscript's current statement — that the observed CDK4/6 group
   mean did not exceed the matched-null mean under any normalization variant — is
   contradicted by `positive_control_variant_summary.csv`, in which `column_minmax` shows
   `direction_observed_gt_null_mean = yes` (0.5888 vs 0.5678). Prespecified criterion 4
   (≥3 of 4 variants) still fails at 1 of 4.
